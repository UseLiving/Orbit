Proof-of-Play

YOUR SCORES ARE THE CHAIN

When a run ends, Orbit takes your score, the game, your wallet and the previous block's hash, then grinds nonces until the SHA-256 hash starts with 00.

Real hashes

Blocks are hashed with SHA-256 in your browser. Each block points at the one before it, so changing an old score breaks every block after it.

A tiny bit of mining

Orbit tries nonces until it finds a hash that starts with 00. That takes about 256 tries, a blink on any device.

Halving every 21 blocks

Your $ORB reward per point halves every 21 blocks on your chain. Early runs pay best, so make them count.
